About Ground Pros LLC

Commercial grounds care built on trust, consistency, and regional expertise.

Our Journey

Founded to keep commercial properties safe, clean, and cared for year-round.

What started as a promise to provide dependable lawn care has become a full exterior property service provider serving landscaping, snow management, parking lot care, and more across Kentucky, Tennessee, Ohio, and Southern Indiana.

We focus on professional project execution, responsive communication, and strong customer relationships so facilities teams can trust every season is handled.
